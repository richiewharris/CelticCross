    How to play

    • The Lord (Host) moves one or two holes in a straight line, any direction. Put him on the summit to win, but only once no Sí piece stands there. Lose him and you lose.
    • Spearmen (Host, 7) step one hole forward, back or sideways.
    • The Chariot (Sí) runs any distance along a row or column.
    • Riders (Sí, 4) step one hole diagonally.
    • The climb. A move that steps up a tier stops there. Downhill is free. Take a piece by moving onto it.
    • The hold. The Sí win if, after seven of their turns in a row, they have a piece on the summit and the Host have none. Any Host piece on the summit resets the count.

    Setup: the Host set their pieces on the outer ring, the Sí anywhere higher, one at a time, Host first. With “Set your rival's”, each side sets the other side's pieces instead, in the same order. Then the Host move first. Taking every Sí piece also wins for the Host; a player with no move loses. If a position comes round a third time, the side with fewer pieces wins. Rules tested over 4,000 computer games; Brandub, the Irish game of five against eight, inspired the pieces and board.
