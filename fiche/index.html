Fiche

A race to twenty with three odd dice: a bone caltrop, a wire-wrapped cube, and two cubes on a rod. Take turns rolling into one shared total. Land on twenty to take the round, but mind the sevens, nines and twelves on the way.

How to play

  • ↻Take turns. Pick one of your dice and roll it; its number goes onto the shared total. Each die is used once a round.
  • 3Roll a three on any die and you roll again, with another die if you have one, or the same one if it was your last.
  • 7Land the total on 7 and you roll again.
  • 9Land on 9 and your opponent takes the round.
  • 12Land on 12 and the round is drawn.
  • 20Land on 20 and you take the round. Go over 20 and you lose it.
  • ⚐If all six dice are used up short of 20, the round is drawn. Whoever rolled last starts the next round.

The caltrop shows the number of notches cut into the leg standing up. The wire cube's number is how many wires run across its top. The twin cubes add the wires on their two tops, 2 to 8, and show 1 only when the bar lands standing on end.
