One revolution of the wheel

The seats stay still; the wheel turns under them, one seat clockwise per step. What is in front of you is what you can touch. Half a turn fills the wheel; the second half brings every enemy card round to you; a full turn brings your first band home and the heroes go to the duels.

1 · The Muster

Sort your deck: 12 cards to hand, the rest to the Reserve you draw from in duels. Omens go face down.

Why: your hand is everything you can place and play before the ford.

2 · Marshalling, 1 of 6

Each of you fills the seat before you: a hero, then two supports to fight beside it, twice.

Why: your first band meets theirs, the opposite slice, in the first duel. You see their hero before choosing its flanks.

4 · The Winnow, 1–6

One action on the enemy card before you: a geis, supplant a support, an omen, an oath on your own hero, or hold.

Why: this is your only chance to spoil their duels before the fighting starts.

5 · Home: the duels

Your first band is back before you. Last omens, then two duels to the death at the chosen setting: opposite slices, first bands then second bands.

Why: fell more heroes than they do. One each? A final duel.
