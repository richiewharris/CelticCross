Names on the wheel

Players never see a spot number. Every place on the wheel is named by whose band it is, which band, and which flank. Duels pair opposite slices.

Band

A pie slice: a hero and the two who fight beside it. Each player raises two, the first band and the second band. Once a hero is placed it can also be called by name: Emer's band.Instead of: quadrant 1–4

Hero · West flank · East flank

The three seats in a band, in the order they come round: the hero, then its two flanks. For now a flank only says where a support stands; rules for flanks can come later.Instead of: spot 1, spot 2, spot 3

Before you

The seat at your front edge of the wheel: the only one you can fill or act on this turn.Instead of: the faced spot

First duel · Second duel

Opposite slices fight: first band against first band, then second against second. The final duel only if each side fells one.Instead of: spots 1 v 7, 4 v 10

Marshalling

The first half turn: six turns, each player filling the seat before them.Instead of: the drafting turn

The Winnow

The second half turn: the enemy's bands come round to you, one seat at a time, to bind, supplant or curse.Instead of: the playing turn

Setting · Muster · Last omens

Kept as they are: they already say what they are.
