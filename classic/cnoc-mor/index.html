      How to play

      • The Lord (Host) steps one hole any way. Stand him on the cairn at the centre to win. On the outer ring he is safe from Riders. Lose him and you lose.
      • Spearmen (Host, 4 + 3 in reserve) step one hole any way, but climb only straight.
      • The Chariot (Sí) runs any distance along a row or column.
      • Riders (Sí, 4 + 1 in reserve) run any distance diagonally.
      • The climb. Four tiers. A move that steps up a tier stops there; downhill is free. Take a piece by moving onto it.
      • Back to the hill. Instead of moving, a Sí piece may jump to any empty summit hole while no Host piece stands on the summit.
      • Reserves. Bring one in as your move: Host spearmen onto the outer ring, the Sí Rider onto the upper slope or summit.

      Setup: the Host set their Lord and 4 spearmen on the outer ring, the Sí their Chariot and 4 Riders on the upper slope or summit, one at a time, Host first. Then the Host move first. The Host also win by taking every Sí piece. The Lord can't take a piece standing on the cairn. A player with no move loses; a position seen a third time goes to the side with fewer pieces; 150 moves each is a draw. Balanced over thousands of computer games: the Host won 47–55% at every search depth tested.
