Goldmines River

Pan a Wicklow stream for gold. Shake the pan so the heavy gold sinks, toss out the stones, then tilt and wash the light gravel over the lip without letting the gold go with it.

    How to pan

    1. Scoop from a spot on the river. Gold drops where the water slows down.
    2. Shake the pan side to side under water. Gold is heavy and sinks; stones and gravel rise.
    3. Pick out the stones once they come to the top.
    4. Wash. Tilt the pan so water carries the top layer over the lip. Dip for more water. Tilt too steep and the gold goes too.
    5. Collect the gold you can see. Anything still buried under sand when you finish is lost.

    In September 1795, gold was found on the slopes of Croghan Kinsella in County Wicklow. For one month, crowds panned the Goldmines River, finding a nugget of about 22 ounces, before soldiers took over the site.
