Celtic Cross Lock

Turn the stones until the knot runs unbroken all the way round. You can only see a third of it at a time, so spin the disc to check your work, then press the center of the dial to try the lock.

How to pick it

  • Tap a stone in the window to give it a quarter turn clockwise. Press and hold (or right-click) to turn it anticlockwise. Each quarter turn uses one turn.
  • Drag the disc, or use ⟲ ⟳, to spin it. Spinning is free. You can also spin it by dragging the center.
  • The lock is solved when the knot has no loose ends anywhere on the disc. A loose end shows as a round knob.
  • Stones with corner rivets are crossings. They're fixed and can't turn.
  • Press the center of the dial to try the lock. If the knot is whole, the shield slides away, the pins drop into their slots and the disc spins free. If it's broken anywhere, the stones scramble and your turns refill.
