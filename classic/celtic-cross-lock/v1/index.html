How to pick it

  • Drag a stone onto another to swap them. You can also tap one stone, then another. Each swap uses a move.
  • The lock opens when every strand meets a strand of the same kind on the next stone, and no strand runs off the edge of the cross.
  • Try the lock whenever you think it's done. If it isn't, the tumblers fall: the stones scramble again and your moves refill.
  • Higher skill gives you more spare moves.
  • Stuck? Turn on Cheat to see broken joins (red notches), stones out of place (red tint) and the next swap to make. A lock opened with Cheat on is practice and doesn't raise your skill.
