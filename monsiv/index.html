Moves

    How to play

    • Throne steps one square in any direction. Lose it and you lose the game. It may never move or swap onto a square where an enemy piece could take it, so it can't capture a guarded piece.
    • Angel steps one square diagonally.
    • Cross steps one square straight: forward, back or sideways.
    • ⇄Swap two of your own different pieces that touch, including diagonally. A swap is your whole turn, and you can't swap the same pair twice in a row.
    • ✕Capture by stepping onto an enemy piece.
    • ↺No repeats. You can't make a move that recreates a position already seen this game, unless it wins.
    • ♛Win by capturing the enemy Throne or by landing any piece on the far row. A player with no legal move loses, so there are no draws.
